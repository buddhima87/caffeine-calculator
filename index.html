How Caffeine Alters Deep Sleep Architecture

Circulating blood alkaloids disrupt the critical macro-architecture of human sleep phases, drastically lessening Slow-Wave Sleep (SWS), widely understood as Deep Sleep. Caffeine operates as a potent stimulant because its molecular dimensions match Adenosine, blinding your nervous system to physiological fatigue.
